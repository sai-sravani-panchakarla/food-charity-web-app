Our Story

What started as a small act of kindness by our family has grown into a monthly tradition. Every month, during the hospital's prenatal checkup days, we cook and serve hot, nutritious meals to pregnant women who travel long distances to visit the doctor.

With the help of our wonderful neighbors who volunteer their time and skills, we prepare wholesome South Indian meals — because every mother deserves to be nourished.
